Who is on stage

A nurse who went looking for what the hospital could not explain.

Krystine St-Laurent spent ten years in critical care before turning to Ayurveda, the oldest living system of health. She has taught it, written about it and lived it since 1989, always with the same question: what does the body already know that we have stopped hearing?

Her Ayurveda trilogy is published by Éditions de l'Homme. Quebec audiences know her from three seasons of Santé la Vie on television, from Salut Bonjour and from her podcast Au-delà des tendances. On stage she is direct, warm and precise, and people leave with something to do the next morning.

  • Registered nurse, ten years in critical care
  • Ayurveda practitioner and teacher since 1989
  • Author of the Ayurveda trilogy, Éditions de l'Homme
  • Host of the podcast Au-delà des tendances

Good to know

Questions organizers ask

What topics does Krystine cover?

She speaks about Ayurveda and women's health, about integrative medicine and the balance between work and life, and about what passes from one generation to the next. Every talk is tailored to the audience.

Does she travel outside Quebec?

Yes. Depending on her schedule she travels across Canada and to the United States, France, Belgium and Switzerland. Give us the city in the form and we confirm feasibility.

In which languages?

French and English, bilingual when the room asks for it.

What are the usual timelines?

Eight to sixteen weeks before the event allows ideal preparation. Tighter requests are reviewed case by case.

What is the fee range?

It varies with the format, the length, the audience and the travel. Tell us about your event in the form and the team replies with a proposal built for it.

Can we record the talk?

Often yes, for internal use. Tell us in the form and the terms are settled with the proposal.
