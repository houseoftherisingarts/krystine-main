Her talks

Three talks, one same path: the return to the body.

Krystine never gives the same talk twice. The titles give the ground. The angle, the examples and the length are then chosen with you, with the room you will have in front of you in mind.

01
Her most requested talk

Beyond trends

We have never had so much information… and never needed our own bearings so much. In this talk, Krystine invites everyone to look inward rather than search outside for the miracle solution, and to listen to what the body already knows, beyond fashions and trends.

60 to 90 minIn person · Virtual
02
Signature talk

Ayurveda as an inner compass

Ayurveda is the oldest living system of health, and Krystine shares it adapted to the reality of our hemisphere. The five elements become a simple map for reading what is going on in you, in your day and in your team. People leave with a few reference points they keep for a long time.

90 min · half dayIn person · Hybrid
03
For women

A woman and her seasons

Just like nature, a woman is governed by cycles, influenced by the seasons, the light and many other factors that Ayurveda brings back to our attention. Krystine offers a way of living in alliance with the body rather than behind its back. For women's groups, health professionals and wellness events.

60 minIn person
Themes

Themes she can address

Depending on the context of the event and the questions running through the audience, Krystine can address, among other things:

  • our ability to choose in a world saturated with answers;
  • the hidden costs of trends, algorithms and universal rules;
  • the body's messages and what sometimes keeps us from hearing them;
  • the rhythms of the body, of nature and of the different cycles of life;
  • Ayurveda as a map, rather than a system of labels;
  • the links between body and consciousness, science and wisdom, nourishing and healing;
  • plants, traditions and the knowledge passed down through generations;
  • the seasons and the passages of women's lives;
  • times of transition, the loss of old reference points and what is seeking to take shape;
  • nourishing, transforming and regenerating;
  • discernment and the place we keep in our own decisions.

Reconnecting what we have learned to separate remains the common thread of all her talks.

In her words

I started in the hospital. Nature did the rest.

As a nurse I worked in intensive care, cardiology, surgery, the operating room and even flight medicine. I saw the best that science can do… and I also understood that science is never final. One day I caught myself counting the years to my retirement, well before forty. That is when plants, and then Ayurveda, came into my life, and an extraordinary world opened up: nature and everything it gives us.

I left the city for a farm, planted gardens of medicinal plants there and created my oils. I wrote, produced and hosted three seasons of the television show Santé ! La Vie !, then my books came out with Éditions de l'Homme, and two of them became best-sellers. What I do on stage is what I do on television and in my pages: simplify the knowledge that lets us cultivate health and vitality every day, and make it accessible to everyone. I deeply believe that each of us is unique, and that the body is made to regenerate.

Good to know

Questions organizers ask

What topics does Krystine cover?

Krystine speaks, among other things, about the body, rhythms, Ayurveda, plants, women's seasons, transitions, discernment and our ability to choose in a world saturated with answers. Each talk draws on close to forty years of research, practice and observation, with one common thread: reconnecting what we have learned to separate.

Does she travel outside Quebec?

Yes! Depending on her calendar, Krystine travels across Canada and also to the United States, France, Belgium or Switzerland. Tell us the city and we confirm quickly.

In which languages?

In French and in English, and in both when the room asks for it.

How much notice is needed?

Eight to sixteen weeks before the event is ideal. For shorter notice, write to us anyway: we look at what is possible.

What are her fees?

They depend on the format, the length, the audience and the travel. Describe your event to us and the team replies with a proposal made for it.

Can we record the talk?

Often yes, for internal use. Tell us in the form and we settle the terms with the proposal.
